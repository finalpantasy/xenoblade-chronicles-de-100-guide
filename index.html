Party Presets

Who you control

Reliable default: Shulk, Chapter 1 to the post-game. His Visions show an incoming enemy art before it lands, and the correct response is Monado Shield, Monado Armour, Monado Speed on the target, or warning the ally with B. The AI cannot make those encounter-specific reactions reliably.

Aggressive alternative from Chapter 8: player-controlled Melia with AI Dunban and Riki. She has the highest Ether ceiling, but AI Melia discharges elementals instead of deliberately banking them. Use the Build Lab above to switch between reliable and fast-kill loadouts.

Why Shulk / Dunban / Riki

Dunban is an evasion tank. With Agility gems and Monado Speed he simply does not get hit by physical attacks. Riki heals with You Can Do It, lands the strongest DoTs, and has a huge HP pool — and his AI plays him well. An evasion tank means almost no incoming damage, so a dedicated healer becomes redundant and battles get much faster. Do not run Reyn and Dunban together — two tanks is redundant unless you are deliberately topple-locking.

Monado Speed on Dunban is the strongest single interaction in the game. An evasion tank with an evasion buff is unhittable by physical attacks.

Skill tree investment order

1. Dunban's agility and evasion skills. The whole comp depends on him not being hit. 2. Shulk's Deadly Determination line for talent gauge generation. 3. Riki's HP and healing. 4. Dunban's Steel Protection before Spike encounters — it reduces Spike damage by 20%, so combine it with the dossier's required mitigation rather than treating it as immunity. 5. Everything else opportunistically.

Leader warning: all 14 finales are badged at their exact route step. Stunted Growth is the strange one: Dunban must accept it, but Shulk must lead for the final turn-in. Melia's Passion tree is not lost if Talia's Research expires; Investigating Satorl is the Chapter 17 replacement prerequisite.

Chapter correction worth knowing. Medical Advancements and Let's Make Fillings! are Chapter 10 quests from the Sacred Altar in Frontier Village, not Chapter 7. The same visit is when you take Mislabelling Problem and Getting Bigger! for Riki's tree — so Frontier Village needs a deliberate return trip in Chapter 10, not just a pass in Chapter 7. Build Central Bionis affinity to 3.5★ in Chapter 7 so the gate is already open when you come back.

Battling Brutes is one of the richest optional bundles and easy to overlook. It is not timed, but it unlocks Fiora's hidden tree, pays 63,500 G and 158,000 EXP, and spawns two Unique Monsters — Firework Geldesia (98) and Reckless Zanden (98) in Tephra Cave's Arachno Queen's Nest. It appears at the Refugee Camp in Chapter 17, which most guides treat as permanently closed after Chapter 5. It is not — Dulland and Elior repopulate it.

Chain warning: Désirée's Future requires The Broken Watch from Chapter 1. Skip the Chapter 1 quest and Shulk's Pessimism tree never becomes available.

Gems

Agility is the strongest stat in the game. It powers the evasion-tank plan and raises hit rate against higher-level enemies, and hit rate is what gates the superbosses.

Gem crafting: the pair of characters working the furnace determines the number of cycles, based on their affinity. Higher party affinity means better gems — another reason the affinity grind pays for itself.

Gear

Gear matters less than gems. A mediocre piece with three good slots beats a better piece with one — prioritise slot count. Do not grind gear during the story; quest rewards and shops are sufficient because your level is capped at the story target. The gear chase belongs in the post-game.

Expert Mode level management

Set your level to the chapter target and leave it. Banked EXP is never lost and there is no penalty for a large bank. Do not spend it during the story — its purpose is the post-game, where the gap between you (~90) and the superbosses (100-120) is otherwise brutal. Do drop your level deliberately for level-conditioned quests and to keep Unique Monster fights meaningful.

Against superbosses, level is only part of it. Abaasy at 120 will still shred an underprepared Lv99 party — gems and Monado Armour matter more than the last few levels.

The farm loop. Skip Travel resets every emptied Collection Point. Empty the points near a landmark, Skip Travel away, come straight back — they have refilled and the rare slot re-rolls. Ten cycles is about five minutes and beats wandering the map every time.

Build order

Housing to 3, then Nature to 3. Almost every resident gates on a Housing level, and Housing 3 + Nature 3 unlocks Berryjammy, who is a hard prerequisite for both Minana and Gowago and who trades Lewisia Silver. Do not attempt any Level 5 until after Chapter 14, since three of the four Level 5 tiers want materials from closing areas.

The 5 superbosses

All are Unique Monsters at Level 100+. Four of the five require clearing Mechonis Core first.

Abaasy is the final check: the route below puts the Night Vision and defensive preparation in place before you deliberately seek its blizzard-night spawn.

Rewards

Rank V crystals with Daze Up and Night Vision attributes, craftable into the Rank VI gems the endgame armour wants. Plus Veritas Glyphs, tradeable for Love Sources, which give large party affinity boosts — the reason superbosses come before the affinity grind.

33 of the 157 Unique Monsters live in permanently-missable areas — Sword Valley 8, Galahad Fortress 3, Mechonis Field 5, Central Factory 9, Agniratha 8. Five of those are double-gated: the quest that spawns them is itself missable. Full list in research/04-unique-monsters.md.
