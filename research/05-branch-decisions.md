# All 10 Mutually Exclusive Quest Pairs — Resolved

Every branching quest in Xenoblade Chronicles DE where taking one permanently kills the other. All ten now have a recommendation with a stated reason.

**Decision principle used throughout:** this is an Expert Mode run capped at story level with EXP banked, targeting a full Affinity Chart and full Collectopaedia. That means **Affinity and reputation beat EXP**, and **gold beats EXP** (Colony 6 costs ~2,000,000 G, EXP is banked and abundant). Where a source says one option gives "better EXP/gold as a later quest" and the other gives "more affinity," affinity wins.

Confidence: ✅ **[V]** two independent sources or explicit reward data. ⚠️ **[S]** single source.

## Detail

### 1. A Little Brother's Fight vs A Big Brother's Fight — Colony 9

Give the pollen to **Jiroque**. Sources state the little brother route is "slightly better for affinity." The choice also determines which follow-up quest becomes available, so it is not purely cosmetic.

### 2. Romantic Notions of a Girl vs Romantic Notions of a Boy — Colony 9, Chapter 3

**⚠️ Corrected.** An earlier version of this file called these "genuinely free" on the strength of a search summary saying there was "no wrong choice." The per-quest wiki pages show that is false — they are mutually exclusive and push the Affinity Chart in **opposite** directions.

| | Romantic Notions of a Girl | Romantic Notions of a Boy |
|---|---|---|
| Giver | **Monica**, Fortress Entrance | **Andreas**, Fortress Entrance |
| Requires | 2★ Colony 9, after the Mechon leave | Girl version **accepted** first |
| Timed | No | **Yes** — dies when you complete the Girl version |
| Task | 1x **Tephra Drop**, near the Spring of Grief in Tephra Cave, **night only** | Identical |
| Reward | 1,200 G · 300 EXP · **Soil Gear** | 1,200 G · 300 EXP · **Soil Boots** |
| Affinity | Monica↔Erik: *Unrequited* → **Mutual Love?**, **+250** | Monica↔Andreas → *Passionate*, +200. **Monica↔Erik degrades to *Parted Ways*** |

**Take the Girl version.** More total affinity, and it is the only one of the two that does not actively damage an existing link.

Quirk worth knowing: the Boy version only appears once the Girl version has been **accepted**, and it expires the moment you **complete** the Girl version. Seeing both in your log at once is normal.

Both are Chapter 3, not Chapter 1 — they need 2★ Colony 9 and the post-Mechon story beat.

### 3. Spirits Raised vs Imaginations Tempered — Refugee Camp

**Spirits Raised.** Better affinity and it unlocks additional Colony 6 quests later. Dies with the Refugee Camp when the relocation begins, so decide before then.

### 4. The Road Home vs To Colony 6! — Colony 6

**The Road Home, in Chapter 5.**

These are not really a choice presented side by side. `The Road Home` is available at the relocation. If you skip it, then once **Otharon leaves for Alcamoth when the party reaches Hidden Machina Village (Chapter 12)**, Dulland offers `To Colony 6!` as a substitute.

| | The Road Home | To Colony 6! |
|---|---|---|
| Available | Chapter 5 | Chapter 12+ |
| Giver | — | Dulland, Colony 6 Main Entrance |
| Task | — | Kill 6 Black Smoke Hox, 6 White Smoke Hox, report to Juju |
| Reward | Lower | 8,800 EXP / 12,000 G / 100 Rep / EXP Up I / AP Up I |
| Outcome | **Identical** — refugees relocate, all their Bionis' Leg quests expire | **Identical** |

The extra reward is trivial. Delaying relocation to Chapter 12 delays Colony 6 population and reconstruction by seven chapters, and reconstruction gates residents → residents gate quests → quests gate the Affinity Chart. Take it early.

### 5. Miss Sweetness Showdown: Berryjammy vs Ma'crish — Colony 6

Both need **3.5★ Colony 6 affinity**, plus `Unstoppable Berryjammy` and `Cook-Off Showdown!` completed, and both require obtaining `The Most Transparent Thing` during the quest. Timed quests; completing one expires the other.

| | Berryjammy | Ma'crish |
|---|---|---|
| EXP | 5,300 | 5,000 |
| Gold | 10,000 | **11,000** |
| Item | Amethyst Leggings | **Amethyst Armour** (chest) |
| Affinity | "Terrible" Berryjammy↔Ma'crish link | Same "terrible" link |

**The Affinity Chart outcome is identical either way**, which removes the usual tiebreaker. Take **Ma'crish** for +1,000 G and the chest piece. If you would rather have the leggings, take Berryjammy; nothing else changes.

### 6. The Melody of Happiness vs Dream of a Poet — Colony 6

Both trigger after `In Pursuit of Love`.

| | The Melody of Happiness | Dream of a Poet |
|---|---|---|
| Giver | **Nic**, south of Front Entrance | **Yura**, near the farm |
| Requires | 2.5★ Colony 6 affinity, after `In Pursuit of Love` | after `In Pursuit of Love` |
| Task | Collect 2 Eks Iron Hearts from Makna Eks | Collect the Swan Pendant, High Entia Tomb B1F |
| Rep | +150 | +150 |
| Gold | 28,500 G | 28,500 G |
| EXP | 24,000 | 24,000 |
| Item | **Rex Arms** | **Rex Greaves** |
| Affinity | Yura link established, **Rosemary → "Good Friends"** | Shulk/Yura dialogue change only |

**Correction:** I originally recommended Melody partly on gold. That reasoning was wrong — **the payouts are identical**. Rep, gold and EXP all match exactly; the only material difference is arms versus greaves.

**The recommendation stands, on affinity alone.** Melody upgrades **Rosemary**, who is an invitable Colony 6 resident (Housing 4), so it feeds reconstruction. Dream of a Poet documents no chart upgrade.

Convenient side effect: Melody hands you 2 Eks Iron Hearts, and **Housing 3 requires an Eks Iron Heart**.

Convenient side effect: it also hands you 2 Eks Iron Hearts, and **Housing 3 needs an Eks Iron Heart**.

### 7. Medical Advancements vs Let's Make Fillings! — Frontier Village

**Medical Advancements.** Better EXP and money. Confirmed by two sources with no counter-argument.

### 8. Talia's Research vs Investigating Satorl — Alcamoth / Eryth Sea

**Talia's Research**, and it is a deadline.

| | Talia's Research | Investigating Satorl |
|---|---|---|
| Giver | **Talia, Alcamoth**, after `A Necessary Upgrade` | Miriall, Ether Plant, Eryth Sea |
| Available | Chapter 8 onward | **Only after clearing Mechonis Core**, and requires `The Missing Boy` accepted |
| Reward | More **Upper Bionis reputation** | More EXP and gold |
| Leads to | `Ancient High Entia Mystery` | `Ancient High Entia Mystery` |

Both unlock `Ancient High Entia Mystery`, which **unlocks Melia's fifth skill branch**, so that is not a differentiator.

Reputation is **Area Affinity**, and Area Affinity is a hard completion requirement: you need 5★ in every area, and the Collectopaedia **"Other" page** is gated behind 3★ and 5★ ratings. EXP is banked and gold is farmable. Reputation is the scarcer resource.

🔴 **Talia is in Alcamoth, which dies at Mechonis Core in Chapter 15.** Do this before then. Note the ugly trap: `Investigating Satorl` is only obtainable *after* Mechonis Core, so if you drift past Chapter 15 without taking Talia's Research, the game silently converts your choice into the worse one.

### 9. Together Forever vs I Love You No Matter What — Alcamoth

**Together Forever.** The choice is not made at these quests at all — it is made earlier, inside **`How Do I Feel?`**:

- Speak to **Rozeal before Ricoth** → route B → **Together Forever** becomes available from Scarlen.
- Speak to **Ricoth before Rozeal** → **I Love You No Matter What**.

Together Forever pays 2,100 EXP / 4,800 G / Physical Protect IV gem (28% drop), and upgrades **two** Affinity Chart links: Scarlen↔Ricoth to "Very Close" and Ricoth↔Rozeal to "Best Friends". The alternative has no documented chart benefit. ⚠️ Single source on the comparison, but two documented upgrades against zero is a clear enough signal.

### 10. Adventurers in Peril vs The Missing Partner — Alcamoth / Valak Mountain

**Adventurers in Peril.**

| | Adventurers in Peril | The Missing Partner |
|---|---|---|
| Giver | Alcamoth | Kurralth, Harict Chapel, Valak Mountain |
| Available | Chapter 8 onward | Later, **only if Adventurers in Peril was not completed** |
| Reward | Lower EXP/gold | Better EXP/gold |
| Affinity | **More affinity** | **Chart does not change**, except a Zain↔Kurralth link if none existed |

A quest that explicitly does not change the Affinity Chart is close to worthless on a 100% chart run. Take Adventurers in Peril. Kurralth then never offers the alternative, which is the correct outcome.
