# All 157 Unique Monsters + 20 Future Connected

Source: RPG Site Unique Monster Locations guide, Game8 Superboss guide.

## Why this matters for 100%

- **Every first kill grants one Affinity Coin to all seven characters.** Affinity Coins are the currency for **Skill Links**, the system that lets a character borrow another character's passive skills. Full Skill Link completion is gated entirely on UM kills.
- 5 superbosses (Lv100+), 8 quest-exclusive spawns.
- **33 UMs live in permanently-missable areas.** Miss them and those Affinity Coins are gone for good.

Those quests are themselves missable. **Double-gated: take the quest AND kill the monster before the area dies.**

## The 5 Superbosses

| Name | Lv | Location | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final Marcus | 100 | Valak Mountain, Three Sage Summit | At night |
| Ancient Daedala | 105 | Fallen Arm, Wreckage Beach | After Mechonis Core |
| Despotic Arsene | 108 | Satorl Marsh, north of Glowing Obelisk | After Mechonis Core |
| Blizzard Belgazas | 114 | Valak Mountain, near Great Glacier | After Mechonis Core |
| Avalanche Abaasy | 120 | Valak Mountain, Three Sage Summit | At night, during blizzard, after Mechonis Core |

**Recommended kill order:** Final Marcus (weakest, dazeable) → Ancient Daedala (farm it for Rank V Night Vision cylinders) → Despotic Arsene → Blizzard Belgazas → Avalanche Abaasy.

Ancient Daedala is the key farm: it drops **Rank V Night Vision cylinders**, and Night Vision is what makes the other superbosses hittable at all. Kill it early in the superboss sequence, then farm it before attempting Abaasy.

**Gem loadout for superbosses**
- **Spike Defence** — mandatory. Near-immunity when paired with Dunban's Steel Protection skill. Marcus, Daedala and Abaasy all have aura damage spikes.
- **Night Vision** — accuracy against higher-level enemies. Maxed rank required for Abaasy.
- **Debuff Resist** or **Divine Protect** — Abaasy has an instant-death counter spike.
- **Topple Plus** — Belgazas has no topple resistance and topple-locking is the intended kill. Rank VI needed to topple-lock Abaasy.
- **Agility Up** — hit rate.
- **Monado Armour** gives 75% party damage reduction. Max it.

**Rewards:** Rank V crystals with Daze Up and Night Vision attributes, craftable into Rank VI gems for endgame armour. Also **Veritas Glyphs**, tradeable for **Love Sources**, which give large party affinity boosts. That makes superbosses a shortcut for the party affinity grind.

**Note:** Ancient Daedala sitting at Fallen Arm *after Mechonis Core* proves Fallen Arm is revisitable late. The Fallen Arm quest lockout at the Agniratha bosses applies to the quests and NPCs, not the area itself. Same pattern as Alcamoth.
