# Colony 6 Reconstruction: Full Bill of Materials + Acquisition

Sources: Xeno Series Wiki, Game8, RPG Site, TheGamer.

## Headline numbers

- **4 categories** (Housing, Commerce, Nature, Special), each level 0 to 5.
- **Total gold: 1,995,000 G.** Housing 503,000 / Commerce 437,000 / Nature 442,000 / Special 613,000.
- All four at Level 5 grants **Titan Plate**. Per-category Level 5 grants its own item (Oriental Glass for Housing, Titan Arms for Commerce).

**Gold is the wall, not materials.** Two million is the largest money sink in the game.

## Risk analysis

### Genuinely at risk (closing area, trade backup is the only recovery)

| Material | Area | Closes at | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Red Frontier** x2 | Sword Valley | Jade Face, Ch13 | 🤝 Rizaka, Hidden Machina Village |
| **Art Core Coil** x2 | Galahad Fortress | Jade Face, Ch13 | 🤝 Kazat, Hidden Machina Village |
| **Mossy Panel** x2 | Mechonis Field | Agniratha bosses, Ch14 | 🤝 Nopo'rikh, Colony 6 |
| **Azure Hollyhock** x2 | Mechonis Field | Agniratha bosses, Ch14 | 🤝 Scarlen, Satorl Marsh (post-MC) |
| **Lewisia Silver** x2 | Agniratha | Meyneth Shrine, Ch14 | 🤝 Berryjammy, Colony 6 |
| **Blue Light Amp** x3 | Agniratha | Meyneth Shrine, Ch14 | 🤝 Oleksiy, Colony 6 |
| **Angel Engine X** x2 | Central Factory | Mechonis Core, Ch15 (revisit Ch16) | 🤝 Shilx, Colony 6 |
| **Sturdy Armour** x4 | Alcamoth trade only | Mechonis Core, Ch15 | ⚔️ Flavel Andos, Eryth Sea (permanent) |
| **Ocean Elixir of Life** x1 | Alcamoth trade | Mechonis Core, Ch15 | ⚔️ Kyel Lexos, Colony 6, rain, post-MC |

**Every single one has a recovery path.** Colony 6 reconstruction cannot be soft-locked. The trade backups just cost affinity and time.

### Not at risk, despite appearances

**Fortune Feather**, **Black Styrene** and **Retro Diode** all appear in the **Colony 6** collection as well as their closing area. Colony 6 never closes.

### The actual grind: three rare collection drops

| Material | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fossil Monkey** x1 | Makna Forest | Needed for Housing 2, very early. Trade backup: Puko |
| **Ice Cabbage** x2 | Valak Mountain | The grindiest item in the game. Trade backup: Yura |
| **Black Liver Bean** x2 | Bionis' Interior | Ch17 only. No trade backup |

### The one item with no trade at all

**Piranhax Fishmeal x5** — Fair Piranhax on Fallen Arm. Farm it or buy Noponstone.

### Weather and location gates

- **Light Rain Element** needs a **thunderstorm** on Bionis' Leg.
- **Ocean Elixir of Life** via Kyel Lexos needs **rain** and post-Mechonis Core.
- **Ponio Hoof Seal** and **Royal Volff Hide** both need **night** on Fallen Arm — one circuit does both.

### Routing consequences

- **Housing is the master key.** Almost every resident gates on a Housing level. Rush Housing, do not build evenly.
- **Berryjammy is the bottleneck:** Housing 3 + Nature 3, and both Minana and Gowago require her. She also trades Lewisia Silver.
- The four Alcamoth residents are **all findable outside Alcamoth**, so they survive Chapter 15.
- The Argentis trio interlock: speak to **both** Zel and En, invite **Don**, then take one twin.

## Strategy

1. **Housing to 3, then Nature to 3**, to unlock Berryjammy early.
2. **Farm materials in the chapter you are already in.** `ROUTE.md` places all 40+ at their natural stop. Coming back at level 85 to kill level 20 mobs is wasted time.
3. **Do not attempt any Level 5 until after Chapter 14**, since three of the four Level 5 tiers want materials from closing areas.
4. **Money, not materials, is what will stall you.** ~2,000,000 G.
