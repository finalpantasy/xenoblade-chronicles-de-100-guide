### Three of these bundle other rewards

1. **Dunban's chain ends in `The Balance of Power`, which spawns two Unique Monsters** — Barbaric Sitri (47) and Banquet Vassago (48), both in Antol Den. One chain, a skill tree and two Affinity Coins.
2. **Melia's Passion tree is safe if `Talia's Research` expires.** `Ancient High Entia Mystery` opens in Chapter 17 after either that quest or `Investigating Satorl`; it also needs 2.5★ Upper Bionis, the High Entia Emblem and Melia leading.
3. **Riki's tree runs through the Frontier Village doctor chain**, the same thread as the `Medical Advancements` vs `Let's Make Fillings!` branch. That chain is not optional filler.

`Désirée's Future` chains from `The Broken Watch` (Ch1). Do the Ch1 quest or the chain never starts.

## 2. The Refugee Camp is NOT permanently dead

**Correction to the earlier missables map.** The Refugee Camp closes for its Chapter 4-5 population when the relocation begins, but **it is repopulated in Chapter 17** with three new quests from Dulland and Elior. These Chapter 17 quests are **not timed**.

| Quest | Giver | Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| `A Flower for a Rose` | Dulland | 38,000 G · 92,500 EXP · Cosmo Drones · Initial Tension VI · +150 Rep |
| **`Battling Brutes`** | Dulland | 63,500 G · 158,000 EXP · Eternity Knives · Speed V Frame · Speed V Booster · **Fiora Skill Tree (Innocence)** · +200 Rep |
| `Supplies for Satorl` | Elior | 45,000 G · 58,000 EXP · Speed V Goggles · Speed V Arms · +100 Rep |

**`Battling Brutes` is triple-value and easy to overlook.** It is not timed, but it unlocks Fiora's hidden skill tree, pays 63,500 G, and spawns two Unique Monsters — `Firework Geldesia` (Lv98) and `Reckless Zanden` (Lv98), both in the Arachno Queen's Nest in Tephra Cave.

## 3. Colony 9's Chapter 1 timed quest block

The entire generic-NPC quest block in Colony 9 Chapter 1 is flagged **timed**:

- Monster Quest 1 (3 parts), Monster Quest 2 (3), Monster Quest 3 (4), Monster Quest 4 (3)
- Challenge 1, 2, 3, 4
- Material Quest 1-4, Collection Quest 1-4, Search Quest 1-4

⚠️ **Expiry point is not stated.** Game8 says only "expire after progressing to a certain part of the game." The authoritative missables lists do not flag Colony 9 quests, which suggests the expiry is late (probably when NPCs relocate at Mechonis Core) rather than at the end of Chapter 1. **Route defensively: clear the whole block in Chapter 1.** It costs an hour and removes the question.

🔴 **Three Unique Monsters are gated behind these Challenge quests:**
- `Challenge 1` → **Evil Rhangrot** (Lv6, Tephra Hill)
- `Challenge 2` → **Verdant Bluchal** (Lv5, beach south of Anti-Air Battery 1)
- `Challenge 3` → **Lake Magdalena** (Lv6, water under Outlook Park)

Also `Challenge 4` → **Wallslide Gwynry** (Lv9) in Tephra Cave, and `The Plan - Execution` → **Dark Murakmor** (Lv18), and `Liliana's Sincere Request` → **Gentle Rodriguez** (Lv40).

## 5. Correction: The Melody of Happiness vs Dream of a Poet

Earlier I recommended Melody partly on gold. **That reasoning was wrong — the payouts are identical.**

| | The Melody of Happiness | Dream of a Poet |
|---|---|---|
| Giver | Nic, south of Front Entrance | Yura, near the farm |
| Rep | +150 | +150 |
| Gold | 28,500 G | 28,500 G |
| EXP | 24,000 | 24,000 |
| Item | **Rex Arms** | **Rex Greaves** |
| Affinity | Yura link + **Rosemary → "Good Friends"** | Shulk/Yura dialogue only |

**Recommendation stands, on affinity alone.** Melody upgrades Rosemary, who is an invitable Colony 6 resident. The only other difference is arms versus greaves.

Prerequisite for both: `In Pursuit of Love` from Rosemary (Ch13, 2F Building) — 25,000 G, 22,500 EXP, Spike Defence V, Pierce Resist V.

Longer than it looked. The surrounding chain is permanent; the two mutually exclusive `Miss Sweetness Showdown` variants are the timed branch point.

## 7. Perrine vs Mefimefi — the recommendation is now stronger

Each unlocks a different late quest:

| Resident | Quest | Chapter | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Perrine** | `Homs Determination` | 13 | +150 Rep · 16,000 G · 9,100 EXP · Arts Heal VI |
| Mefimefi | `Nopon Determination` | 17 | +150 Rep · 48,000 G · 72,000 EXP · Arts Heal VI |

Mefimefi's pays far more. `Homs Determination` instead requires the defeat of **Territorial Rotbart** (Lv81) on Gaur Plain — a Unique Monster and therefore an Affinity Coin clear.

**Route recommendation: take Perrine.** It keeps that late-game Unique Monster objective explicit in the route. This is not evidence that the quest spawns Rotbart or makes its Affinity Coin exclusive. Take Mefimefi only if the higher `Nopon Determination` reward is your preferred outcome; the residents and their quests are mutually exclusive.

## 8. Colony 6's timed quest load

Almost every Colony 6 Reconstruction HQ quest is timed. Do not bank them.

**Ch6:** What is Love?!, What is Courage?! *(spawns Elegant Marin, Lv29)*, Matryona's Answer, A Selfish Girl's Mistake, Chemist's Reopening, Satata's Younger Brother, Rest in Peace, For the Restoration, Making a New Path
**Ch7:** A Gutsy Trader, Defend Colony 6 - Mechon
**Ch8:** the Cook-Off chain, A Tantalising Treat, Missing Lodger *(recruits Nopo'rikh)*, Defend Colony 6 - Reptile
**Ch10:** Cook-Off Final Blow?!, A Delectable Delicacy, Looking for Freedom, the Berryjammy chain, Miss Sweetness Showdown, The Most Transparent Thing
**Ch13:** Defend Colony 6 - Elite
**Ch4:** Secret Mission (Daza, Watchpoint Junction)

`For the Restoration` and `Making a New Path` additionally require **Colony 6 reconstruction completion**, so they gate on your build progress as well as the clock.

Five quests from **Vanea on the Junks Ship**, 12,000 EXP each:

`Replica Monado 1` → **Monado Rudra** · `2` → **Monado Agni** · `3` → **Monado Abyss** · `4` → **Monado Dogma** · `5` → **Monado Saga**
