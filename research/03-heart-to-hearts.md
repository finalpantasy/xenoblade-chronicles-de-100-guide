# All 63 Heart-to-Hearts

Source: RPG Site Heart-to-Heart Locations & Answers Guide, cross-checked against Xeno Series Wiki.

## Rules

- **63 total.** 7 characters = 21 pairs, 3 Heart-to-Hearts each.
- **None are missable.** Confirmed. They can be viewed at any affinity at or above the requirement, and areas affected by late story events still allow them.
- **Alcamoth stays usable.** After the Mechonis Core events you can no longer Skip Travel in, but you can walk in from the **Centre Gate at Eryth Sea**. The NPCs are gone, so quests die, but the Heart-to-Hearts do not. Some require warps repaired by late-game quests.
- Many require story progress before they appear, independent of affinity.
- Affinity thresholds run **Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple → Pink**, pink being highest.
- Both answers listed below are the max-affinity answers. Wrong answers still complete the scene but give less party affinity.

**Routing note:** because nothing here is missable, do not distort the route to chase Heart-to-Hearts. Run them in bulk during the mid-game affinity push and again after the party is complete, when pink-tier ones become reachable. The only real constraint is that Fiora rejoins late, so all Fiora pairs above green stack up in the back third.
